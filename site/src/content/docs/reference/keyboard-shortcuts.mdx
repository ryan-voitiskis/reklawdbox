## Performance mode

Performance mode ships with two presets. To switch between them, open **Preferences > Keyboard** while in Performance mode -- the preset dropdown appears at the top of the panel.

- **Performance 1** (default) -- 4 hot cues per deck, 1/2 to 8 beat loops.
- **Performance 2** -- 5 hot cues, 1/8 to 2 beat loops, full reverse, tempo mode switching.

You can also start from **None** and build your own. Any preset can be further customized, exported, and imported.

<Aside type="tip">
Hold <kbd>Option</kbd> with any Deck 1 or Deck 2 shortcut to control **Deck 3** or **Deck 4**.
</Aside>

## Notes

- **No keyboard shortcuts exist for:** EQ bands, channel faders, crossfader, RMX effects, or STEMS isolation. These require a hardware controller.
- **FX controls** (Beat FX select, halve/double beats, on/off, dry/wet) appear in Preferences > Keyboard but have **no default key assignments**. You can assign them manually.
- **Search shortcut quirk:** In Performance mode, Search uses <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> (not <kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd>). In Export mode, it uses <kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd>.
