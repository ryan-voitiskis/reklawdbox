## Outcome

Order at least two locked chapters, find bridge tracks between them, refine any
internal sequences, and optionally export the approved full plan for Rekordbox.

<WorkflowContract id="chapter-set-planning" />

## Phases

1. **Define the chapters.** Start from playlists already built and validated
   with [Pool Building](/workflows/pool-building/), and mark each one as fixed
   sequence or unordered improvisation.
2. **Shape the night.** Compare chapter orders against the intended overall
   energy arc and choose boundary tracks where needed.
3. **Find and judge bridges.** Review one to three connecting tracks for each
   boundary, then refine any chapter sequence that makes the transition weak.
4. **Approve and export.** Confirm chapter order, bridges, internal sequences,
   and unrelated staged metadata before creating optional playlist XML.

## Decisions and approval checkpoints

- A **chapter** is a locked pool you have validated. A **bridge** is a short run
  that connects one chapter's boundary to the next. The **set plan** combines
  chapter order, bridges, and the overall energy arc.
- At least two chapter playlists and a sequenced/unordered decision for each are
  hard requirements. Missing BPM, key, genre, or optional analysis weakens the
  relevant scoring axis rather than blocking the plan.
- Stratum can contribute BPM/key evidence; Essentia improves energy,
  brightness, rhythm, and timbral evidence but remains optional.
- Approve every bridge and any proposed internal reordering before the plan is
  treated as complete.

Use [Set Building](/workflows/set-building/) for one fixed sequence and
[Pool Building](/workflows/pool-building/) when you only need a compatible
unordered crate.

## What changes and how to recover

The plan lives in the conversation rather than a durable cursor. Chapter
playlists persist, but ordering and bridge choices must be reconstructed after
a restart. Change the chapter order, boundary tracks, or scoring preferences
and rerun only the affected bridge search when a transition fails. Timbral
normalization or saved presets may persist locally.

Playlist export also writes and clears unrelated staged metadata, so inspect
`preview_changes` first and complete its separate metadata handoff when needed.
Export fails closed unless the built-in database backup succeeds or the
configured custom backup script exits zero. A failed export restores staged
metadata for retry.

## Finish in Rekordbox

Keep every exported XML and follow the
[playlist XML import steps](/reference/xml-export/#importing-into-rekordbox).
Verify each imported playlist's name, count, and order—including chapter
boundaries—before cleanup. Use the separate All Tracks flow for any included
staged metadata.
