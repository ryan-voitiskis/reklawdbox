Process newly acquired music into a tagged, organized structure for Rekordbox import. Handles albums, loose tracks, and zip archives from any source.

**Paste into your agent to start:**

```
Process the new music in [/path/to/folder] for Rekordbox import.
```

<Aside type="note">
  This workflow handles Artist, Title, Album, Year, Track Number, Label, and cover art. Genre tagging is handled separately by the [genre classification workflow](/workflows/genre-classification/) after import.
</Aside>

## What this workflow does

1. **Extracts and organizes** — unzips archives, categorizes albums vs loose tracks
2. **Enriches metadata** — Discogs/Beatport lookups for label, year, artist verification
3. **Writes tags** — all required fields including WAV dual-tagging
4. **Renames files** — `NN Artist - Title.ext` convention
5. **Embeds cover art** — from included images or Discogs
6. **Moves to final location** — organized by artist/album with year

<Aside type="caution">
  Rekordbox reads **only RIFF INFO** from WAVs. `write_file_tags` writes both ID3v2 and RIFF INFO automatically.
</Aside>
