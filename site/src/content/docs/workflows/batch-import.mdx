Processes newly acquired music — albums, loose tracks, zip archives — into a properly tagged and organized structure for Rekordbox import.

Paste into your agent:

```
Process the new music in [/path/to/folder] for Rekordbox import.
```

Genre tagging is handled separately by [Genre Classification](/workflows/genre-classification/) after import.

## What it does

1. Extracts and organizes (unzips archives, sorts albums vs loose tracks)
2. Enriches metadata from Discogs/Beatport (label, year, artist verification)
3. Writes tags to files (including WAV dual-tagging for Rekordbox compatibility)
4. Renames files to a consistent convention
5. Embeds cover art from included images or Discogs
6. Moves everything to its final location
