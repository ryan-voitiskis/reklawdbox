Classifies ungenred tracks using evidence from Discogs, Beatport, label mappings, and audio analysis. Results are grouped by confidence level and every change needs your approval.

Paste into your agent:

```
Classify genres for my ungenred tracks.
```

## How it works

The agent runs a genre decision tree against all your cached evidence — Discogs styles, Beatport genres, label associations, BPM, and audio features. Results come back grouped by confidence:

- **High** — multiple sources agree and BPM is plausible
- **Medium** — partial agreement or single strong source
- **Low** — weak or ambiguous evidence
- **Insufficient** — conflicting data, needs a manual call

High and medium confidence results can usually be approved in bulk. Low and insufficient need a closer look.

## Prerequisites

All enrichment providers should be at 100% cache coverage. If you're following [Library Cleanup](/workflows/library-cleanup/), Session 2 (enrichment) handles this.

## Genre taxonomy

The built-in taxonomy has ~50 genres, tuned for DJ mixing — specific where it matters (Deep House and Afro House play very differently) without being overly granular. It's a starting point, not a closed list. You can add genres during classification when the evidence supports it.
