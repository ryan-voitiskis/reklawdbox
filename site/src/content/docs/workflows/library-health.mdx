## What happens next

1. **Confirm access.** Connect to the intended Rekordbox library and make sure
   every content root you want checked is readable.
2. **Scan structure.** Check broken links, files on disk but absent from
   Rekordbox, tracks missing from playlists, and metadata or exact-file
   duplicate groups.
3. **Review the evidence.** Separate credible relocation suggestions and true
   duplicates from intentional copies, archives, or unassigned tracks.
4. **Act outside the scan.** Make only the manual Rekordbox or filesystem
   changes you approve, then rerun the affected scans.

## Decisions and approval checkpoints

- Run this before [Library Cleanup](/workflows/library-cleanup/), after moving
  files or importing a large batch, or as periodic maintenance.
- The scan needs no enrichment, cache coverage, network access, or approval.
- Review every recommendation before relocating, importing, assigning, or
  deleting anything. Similar artist/title text is not proof that two tracks are
  interchangeable.

## What changes and how to recover

The health workflow writes no metadata, files, caches, or workflow state. Its
results reflect the library and filesystem at scan time. After changing the
scope, moving files, or making a manual fix, rerun the affected checks before
trusting the new totals. If a root is unreadable, correct access or narrow the
scope first.

## Finish in Rekordbox

Relocate confirmed broken links, import or remove confirmed orphans, assign
tracks you intend to keep, and review duplicate removal in Rekordbox. The scan
does not perform those actions for you. Rerun the relevant check and compare
counts before declaring the issue resolved.
